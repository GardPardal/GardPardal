I turn ad spend into measured revenue. For **10+ years** I've been selling online and running paid acquisition, with **R$10M+ (~US$1.8M)** managed on Meta and Google. Through Orvan I've served **100+ businesses**, and at V4 Company I ran campaigns for brands such as C6 Bank, TIM, Spotify and Emagrecentro.

What makes my work different: I build the **whole acquisition chain** myself — offer, ads, funnel, tracking and the website that converts it.

### What I do
- **Paid media** — Meta Ads & Google Ads planned and scaled for profit and ROAS
- **Tracking & attribution** — Pixel + Conversions API, GTM, GA4, server-side events, CRM attribution
- **Conversion websites** — fast, SEO-first sites and landing pages (Cloudflare Workers, Node, PHP)
- **AI & automation** — WhatsApp assistants, automated reporting, the Orvan Traffic Robot
